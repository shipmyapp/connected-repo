## What we collect and why

| Data | Why | Kept |
|---|---|---|
| Sign-in identity from Google or Apple (name, email, picture, provider account id) | To sign you in | While your account exists |
| Your profile settings (time zone, theme, reminder times) | To run the app as you set it | While your account exists |
| Content you create (journal entries, attached files) | To show it to you and your team | Until you delete it or your account |
| Team memberships and invitations (email or phone of people you invite) | To share with your team | While the team exists |
| Sessions (IP address, browser, operating system, device) | Security, "Where you're signed in", rate limits | Up to 30 days per session |
| Your devices: for each phone, tablet or computer you use, its kind, model, browser, operating system and version, screen size, time zone and language; whether the app is installed there and when it was last opened installed; whether notifications are allowed there; its push token; the app's install offers there and what came of them; a random device label kept in the `app_device` cookie (up to 400 days, renewed while used, never used to sign in) | To offer the app and ask for notifications only where they are missing, to send notifications to the devices you turned them on for, and to recognise an iPhone's Safari and its Home Screen app as one phone (matched from these few facts, never from your position or anything you type) | While your account exists |
| Connected apps (which AI apps you connected, the access you granted, what each changed) | To let you see and end each connection | While connected; the record of changes while your account exists |
| Error reports (your account id, never your content; screen recordings masked) | To fix bugs | TODO: retention |
| Your agreement to these texts (version, time) | To show what you agreed to | While your account exists |

## Cookies and local storage

- The session cookies, to keep you signed in.
- The `app_device` cookie described above.
- The app's offline copy of your data on your device (IndexedDB and the browser's file storage), so it works without a connection. It is removed from the device when you delete your account there, and from your other devices the next time they open the app online after the account is deleted.

## Your rights

Download your data from Profile → Download my data. Delete your account from Profile → Delete my account (after leaving or deleting your teams; 30-day grace, during which you can keep it). See and end your sessions from Profile → Where you're signed in. For anything else, write to the privacy contact. TODO: the authority you can complain to.

## What deletion removes and what stays

Removed: your account, profile, sessions, devices, connected apps, your journal entries and files (also the copies in file storage), your personal team. Stays: teams you created and left stay with their members (without your name as creator); invitations other teams sent to your email stay with those teams; the operators' record of actions they took on your account (by account id only).
